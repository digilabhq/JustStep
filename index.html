Weight Tracking Tips
  • Weigh yourself weekly, not daily

  • Same time each week (morning is best)

Track Activity
Ways to log activities:

• QUICK LOG: Tap a chip for activities you log often — they appear automatically

• BROWSE: Pick anything from the category list below the chips

• DETAILED LOG: Inside a workout day, track a specific exercise with reps (e.g., Bicep Curls: 20 reps)

Each activity saves the moment you confirm it — tap Undo on the popup if you make a mistake. Tap any logged entry to edit its value, or × to remove it.

Fewer taps, same tracking!
